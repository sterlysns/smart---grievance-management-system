How GrievanceAI Works

A simple next-step process from complaint to resolution.

01

Tell Us Your Problem

Write your grievance naturally and add your area, landmark and pincode. You can optionally attach supporting evidence.

02

AI Understands It

The system analyzes keywords and context to identify the likely complaint category and responsible department.

03

Route to the Right Office

Location information is used in this demo to suggest the relevant local office and verified contact details.

04

Track Every Stage

After submission, you receive a grievance ID and can follow Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved.
